# Brand messaging and claim boundaries

## Safe core message

Describe WATCHWORLDCUP as an IPTV and M3U subscription service that asks users to confirm current content, territory, language, device, app, delivery and terms before payment. Keep editorial resources clearly separate from commercial availability.

## Evidence required before publication

- Rights, channel, film, series, PPV and territory availability
- Catalog totals, resolution, uptime, buffering and activation claims
- Payment providers, free trials, refunds, cancellation and compatibility guarantees
- Ratings, reviews, customer totals, awards and market-leadership statements
- Any FIFA, league, broadcaster, studio or platform relationship

Record approved evidence in the [rights and availability register](../rights-evidence-register.md). If evidence is missing, state the limitation or remove the claim; do not replace it with a guess, testimonial or marketing superlative.
