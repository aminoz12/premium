# Brand Authority & Knowledge Panel Strategy

This document outlines the roadmap for securing Google Knowledge Panels, entity verification, and managing brand search results for **WATCHWORLDCUP**.

## 1. Knowledge Panel Triggers

To trigger a Google Knowledge Panel:
- Maintain consistent Organization schema markup on `https://watchworldcup.us`.
- Reference verified brand attributes: Name (`WATCHWORLDCUP`), Legal Name (`WATCHWORLDCUP Digital Media`), Logo (`https://watchworldcup.us/logo.svg`), and Founding Year (`2026`).
- Ensure consistent SameAs links in Organization JSON-LD linking to `/about` and `/editorial-policy`.

## 2. Managing Visual SERP Elements

- **Sitelinks Search Box**: Provide WebSite schema with `potentialAction` search target.
- **Brand Emblem & Logo**: Serve explicit SVG logo URL in Schema.org `logo` field to allow Google to index `logo.svg` as the official brand icon.
- **Social & Contact Cards**: Provide direct WhatsApp support link (`+212723279328`) for instant user trust.
