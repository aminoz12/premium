# On-page publication checklist

- [ ] A completed content brief identifies the user question, intent, owner, evidence and indexability decision.
- [ ] The page has one H1, a unique accurate title/description, self-canonical URL and route-specific social image.
- [ ] The opening answers the question and gives relevant date, territory and limitations.
- [ ] Every material factual or commercial claim has a source or an evidence-register entry.
- [ ] Related internal pages are linked naturally; no private, obsolete or redirecting URL is used.
- [ ] Schema matches visible content and has no unsupported rating, product, offer, review or affiliation claim.
- [ ] Images meet the image policy and all interactions are keyboard accessible.
- [ ] The sitemap, `llms.txt`, RSS feed and redirect policy are updated only when the route warrants it.
- [ ] `npm run release:check` passes before deployment.
