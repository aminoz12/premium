# GEO citation and evidence policy

## Source order

1. First-party owner-verified service facts for brand, purpose, prices and contact destination.
2. Primary organizers, regulators, manufacturers and platform documentation for external facts.
3. Directly observed, dated test results with reproducible methods.
4. Clearly labeled calculations derived from published source tables.

Do not use anonymous aggregators to broaden commercial, rights, availability or affiliation claims.

## Citation-ready content

A passage is citation-ready when it provides:

- a direct answer near the relevant heading;
- the entity or subject named explicitly;
- date, territory and scope where they matter;
- a visible source link or first-party verification basis;
- limitations and exceptions next to the claim;
- matching structured data only when supported by visible content.

## Commercial boundaries

Named services such as beIN SPORTS, Disney or Netflix are customer-request examples unless current availability is confirmed directly. Their appearance does not establish inclusion, resale rights, endorsement, partnership or official status.

Plan prices are fixed at $25 for 3 months, $38 for 6 months and $62 for 1 year. No other price, payment method, inventory total, quality promise or availability guarantee is publishable without verified evidence.

## Corrections

Material errors must be reportable through the Corrections page. Source updates must preserve the prior evidence record and update visible dates, datasets and structured data together.

GEO visibility, citations and generated answers cannot be guaranteed.
