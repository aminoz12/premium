# SEO blockers requiring owner evidence

Do not publish or mark up the following commercial claims until the owner provides a dated evidence record that identifies the territory, source, approver, review date and expiry date.

| Claim class | Status | Required evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Content/channel, PPV, film and series availability | Blocked | Written rights/availability record by territory and current inventory source |
| Catalog totals, including channel and VOD counts | Blocked | Dated inventory export and responsible owner |
| Resolution, uptime, buffering, activation and device guarantees | Blocked | Reproducible operational measurements or support policy |
| Free trials, payment methods, refunds and cancellation terms | Blocked | Current written commercial policy and fulfilment evidence |
| Ratings, reviews, customer totals and "best" claims | Blocked | Authentic, visible review data and an eligible markup basis |
| FIFA, league, broadcaster, studio or platform affiliation | Blocked | Written authorization; otherwise retain the non-affiliation disclosure |

Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, analytics access and legal review are also external prerequisites for measuring indexing, performance and commercial compliance.
